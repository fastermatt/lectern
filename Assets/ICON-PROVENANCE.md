# Lectern icon — 0.17

Created 2026-10-28 with the built-in image-generation tool, editing the existing Lectern L/bookmark icon. This is a generated raster asset, not a hand-drawn vector. Prompt: preserve the L and bookmark; remove orange dot, rubber/fabric textures, inset channels and heavy bevels; use graphite #242622, warm ivory #F7F4EC and muted amber; orthographic front view with transparent exterior and a legible small silhouette. The result has subtle shading rather than mathematically exact flat color fills.

Original generated image is retained at `work/reception-buildout/generated-icon.png`; previous app assets at `work/reception-buildout/previous-icon`. `sips` makes the 1024px master and macOS iconset sizes; `iconutil` builds AppIcon.icns. Inspected at master and 32px sizes. The generated artifact is a design choice for this requested update, not an assertion that the older draft prohibition on generated brand art was adopted.
