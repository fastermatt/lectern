The Belgic Confession (1561, revised 1619)

Public domain. Text: the Reformed (Dutch) Church in America's 19th-century
authorized English translation, read from
https://thewestminsterstandard.org/the-belgic-confession/ — verified as the same
translation Philip Schaff reprinted in Creeds of Christendom, vol. III (1877; Baker
1889 reprint), whose CCEL plain-text export explicitly states "Rights: Public
Domain" (https://ccel.org/ccel/s/schaff/creeds3/cache/creeds3.txt).

Proof texts: primarily Schaff's own footnoted Scripture-proof apparatus (316
footnotes across the 37 articles) from that same public-domain volume, plus this
translation's own inline citations, merged.

IMPORTANT — a candidate source was checked and REJECTED for this document:
NonlinearFruit/Creeds.json's Belgic Confession text was found, on direct comparison,
to be verbatim the Christian Reformed Church's modern official translation
(crcna.org/sites/default/files/BelgicConfession.pdf), which is explicitly
copyrighted by Faith Alive Christian Resources (Synod 2011) and restricts
reproduction beyond 100 study/worship copies. That text is NOT used anywhere in
this app. See scripts/standards-sources/SOURCES.md for the full comparison and
evidence trail.
