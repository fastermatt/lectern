Rights correction reviewed 2026-10-27 (Codex). The filename is retained for
compatibility; it is not a blanket public-domain claim. The database includes
both public-domain material and excerpts from copyrighted translations.
Redistribution needs review per work and translation. Presence in a private
beta does not establish unrestricted publication permission. Historical or
theological admission criteria do not establish copyright status.
