The Heidelberg Catechism (1563)

Public domain. Traditional English translation with its lettered Scripture-proof
apparatus, verified word-for-word identical at two independent sites:
  https://www.ccel.org/creeds/heidelberg-cat.html (Christian Classics Ethereal
  Library; used as the parse source)
  https://thewestminsterstandard.org/heidelberg-catechism/ (cross-check)

Deliberately NOT the modern 1975/1988/2011 joint CRC & RCA translation, which is
copyrighted by Faith Alive Christian Resources and reads "belong-body and soul, in
life and in death-to my faithful Savior, Jesus Christ." This translation's archaic
diction ("thy", "sincerely willing and ready, henceforth, to live unto him") is
textually distinct and pre-dates that modern copyrighted revision.
