Verified 2026-09-22 to be the unabridged Commentary on the Whole Bible (not the Concise
Commentary this app previously bundled): sample chapters (Genesis 1, Romans 8, Psalm 119,
Isaiah 53, Revelation 1, John 3) carry the full-length book introductions and extended
verse-by-verse exposition characteristic of the Complete edition, not the Concise's brief
"practical observations" format.

Coverage note: this digitization spans 65 of the 66 canonical books (verified against the
commentary's own books.json: numberOfBooks 65, missing Song of Solomon). Matthew Henry
(1662-1714) completed Genesis through Acts himself; Romans onward was finished after his
death by other nonconformist ministers from his notes, per the work's well-documented
publishing history -- long issued and treated as one continuous "Complete Commentary," as
it is here.
