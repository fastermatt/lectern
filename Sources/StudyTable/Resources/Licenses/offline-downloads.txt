Tested and NOT added on 2026-10-31: Douay-Rheims 1899 (engDRA) and the Clementine
Vulgate (latVUC) fail the 66-book/1189-chapter validation because their Vulgate book
and chapter structure (e.g. Daniel 13–14) differs; they need explicit versification
mapping first. BBE remains excluded pending the edition/jurisdiction research below.

Attribution is retained in installed manifests and displayed with Compare text.
Rendering strips USFM markup and footnotes and retains only the 66-book Protestant
canon. Individual editions may differ in verse numbering or textual readings;
reference matching is by book/chapter/verse, not semantic alignment. Validation
requires all 66 books, standard chapter counts (1189 total), at least 30,000 verses,
and nonempty text. This is structural validation, not an editorial certification.
No theological approval is implied by inclusion of a translation.

Deliberately excluded: ESV/NASB/NKJV (no complete offline rights); Douay-Rheims
(Vulgate numbering would require explicit reference remapping); French Darby 2024
(source expressly uses Hebrew verse numbering); draft French/Spanish Free Bible
editions (draft status); partial NT-only editions; BBE (edition/jurisdiction research
still needed). More editions should be added only after source licence AND complete
live installation/versification checks, not merely by guessing an eBible identifier.

Downloading needs internet; reading installed text does not. No AI or account used.
Archive limits: 30 MiB compressed, 80 MiB expanded, 8 MiB/member, 500 members.
ZIP paths are validated; nothing is extracted to an archive-supplied pathname.
Managed packs are installed by same-volume atomic rename. Unmanaged and bundled
packs are never overwritten or removed through the download manager.
