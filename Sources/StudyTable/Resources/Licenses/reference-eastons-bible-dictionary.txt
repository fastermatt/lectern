Throughline Bible — Reference licence record: Easton's Bible Dictionary
====================================================================

Content: 3,961 dictionary articles (names, places, terms), plus a book-
introduction mapping for all 66 canon books. Used to build
`Resources/Reference/easton/**`.

Original work: Matthew George Easton, M.A., D.D., "Easton's Bible
Dictionary" (Illustrated Bible Dictionary, Third Edition), published by
Thomas Nelson, 1897 (public domain; author died 1894/his final edition
published posthumously in 1897 — over a century past any copyright term).

Digital edition used: the official CrossWire SWORD Project module "Easton"
(module version 2.0.1, 2022-08-03), downloaded from
  https://www.crosswire.org/ftpmirror/pub/sword/packages/rawzip/Easton.zip
Cached at scripts/reference-sources/Easton.zip (and its extracted
mods.d/easton.conf at scripts/reference-sources/easton.conf).

Licence evidence — quoted verbatim from the module's own mods.d/easton.conf:

    DistributionLicense=Public Domain
    TextSource=CCEL

    About=Easton's 1897 Bible Dictionary\par\par Public Domain -- Copy
    Freely\par\par These Dictionary topics are from M.G. Easton M.A., D.D.,
    Illustrated Bible Dictionary, Third Edition, published by Thomas
    Nelson, 1897. Due to the nature of etext, the illustrated portions of
    the Dictionary have not been included.

Format note: same SWORD "zLD" binary format and the same pure-Python reader
as Nave's Topical Bible — see reference-naves-topical-bible.txt in this
folder for the format/provenance note.

Known upstream data-quality note (documented, not silently "fixed"): a
small number of articles containing shorthand comparative citations (e.g.
"Titus, Epistle to", comparing Titus and 1 Timothy verse by verse) have a
handful of osisRef citations mis-tagged to Genesis 1 by the CCEL/CrossWire
TEI conversion itself — a pre-existing defect in the upstream digital
edition, not introduced by this build. Spot-checked: 230 articles cite
Genesis 1 at all, the overwhelming majority correctly (CREATION, MAN, HERB,
HEAVEN...); "Titus, Epistle to" is a confirmed miscite. Not auto-corrected,
per this project's standing rule against fragile broad "fix" heuristics for
isolated upstream defects (see the Athanasius/Song of Solomon precedent in
the project wiki, 0.6.1).
