The Canons of the Synod of Dort (1618-1619) — Positive Articles only

Public domain. The Reformed (Dutch) Church in America's authorized translation, as
reprinted by Philip Schaff (Creeds of Christendom, vol. III, 1877/1889 — CCEL:
"Rights: Public Domain"); verified word-for-word identical between
NonlinearFruit/Creeds.json's copy and Schaff's own reprint.

Proof texts: this translation's own inline Scripture quotations/citations (this
document's historic proof-text apparatus is inline, not a separate footnote list).

WITHHELD FROM THIS BUILD: the Rejection of Errors (34 paragraphs). Not present in
Schaff's reprint (his own editorial note states the RCA Constitution he reprinted
from prints only the positive Articles). The only source found was the United
Reformed Churches in North America's own published confessional-standard page
(urcna.org/urcna/confessionshtml/canonshtml/), which carries no copyright notice —
but on review that is an absence of a restriction, not positive evidence of public-
domain status, and the wording reads like the mid-20th-century CRC Psalter Hymnal
rendering of the Canons, whose actual US copyright/renewal status could not be
confirmed. Per this project's licensing gate ("if a source is unclear, do not ship
it"), the Rejection of Errors is withheld from 0.7.0. The parsing code is kept (not
deleted) in scripts/build-standards.py's build_canons(), which never emits these
items into cd.json. Candidate public-domain replacements for a future pass: Thomas
Scott's 1818 English translation of the Acts of the Synod of Dort (pre-1929,
unambiguously PD, includes the Rejection of Errors in full), or any other verified
pre-1931 US edition. Full detail: scripts/standards-sources/SOURCES.md.
