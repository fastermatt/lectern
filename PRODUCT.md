# Product principles

Thruline makes serious Bible study available without a required payment, account,
network connection or AI model. Scripture is the primary reading surface; linked
sources and notes help the reader understand a passage without overwhelming it.

Thruline does not declare one fixed confessional identity. Readers can choose an
alignment lens or none. Historical reception, exact quotations, editorial summaries
and disputed interpretations remain distinct, with evidence and edition information.
No AI output or author-wide assumption is a substitute for human source review.

The interface uses restrained paper/metal surfaces, compact controls, editable
palettes, complete light/dark modes and accessible labels. Avoid decorative controls
that consume study space. Preserve local data compatibility and deliberate sharing.

The current release is a beta, not a promise of full commercial-software parity.
Resource coverage, languages, rights and dataset alignment have documented limits.
