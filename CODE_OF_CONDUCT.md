# Community conduct

Treat people with care, including across languages, cultures and Christian traditions.
Challenge claims with evidence; do not attack a person's character or assign motives.
No harassment, threats, doxxing, discriminatory abuse, spam or unwanted contact.
Disagreement over theology is allowed; humiliation and personal targeting are not.
Respect privacy and copyright. Avoid posting private notes, contact details or keys.

Maintainers may hide harmful content, ask for revisions, temporarily restrict activity
or remove access, proportionate to the behavior. Explain non-sensitive decisions and
allow an appeal to an uninvolved maintainer when available. No response-time guarantee
or independent moderation team is implied before people accept those roles.

Use GitHub's private Report content/Report abuse controls for sensitive harassment
reports. Do not post identifying details in a public issue. For ordinary process
questions, open an issue. Repository maintainers must establish a private community
contact before advertising a staffed moderation service.
