# Historic works — labels and topic guide (review of 2026-10-31)

Prepared for 0.22 by three parallel reviews: a patristics historian (era, genre, topics,
cautions, last-things positions), a historical theologian (topic guide for a pastor's
question about dispensational distinctives), and an ecumenical editor (Catholic,
Orthodox and Protestant reception, edition notes, reading level). Every caution in
`Sources/StudyTable/HistoricWorkLabels.swift` carries a source link. Labels describe;
they are not verdicts. Chapter numbers were checked against online editions and may
differ slightly in the bundled/downloaded editions (Papias fragments, Hermas
sub-sections, Legge's Refutation).

## Topic guide — where to read (in the library)
**Thousand years (Rev 20).** Barnabas 15; Papias fragments; Ayer Period II §10
"Chiliastic Expectations" (Papias, Irenaeus AH V.33, Justin Dial. 80–81) and §47
(Dionysius v. Nepos); Origen, De Principiis II.11.2; Augustine, City of God XX.6–9, XX.13;
Calvin, Institutes III.25.5.
**Caught up (1 Thess 4:17) and the tribulation.** City of God XX.20; Hermas Vision II.2,
IV.1–3 (the great tribulation believers endure); Barnabas 4; 1 Clement 23.
**Antichrist.** Barnabas 4; Polycarp, Philippians 7; Hermas Vision IV; Contra Celsum
VI.45–46; City of God XVIII.52–53, XX.11, 13, 19, 23; Calvin IV.2.12, IV.7.25.
**Israel and the church.** Barnabas 4, 9, 13–14 (Ayer calls it extreme); Ignatius,
Magnesians 8–10; Diognetus 3–6; Origen, De Principiis IV.1.21–22; City of God
XVIII.46–47, XX.29; Calvin IV.16.14–15, II.10–11.
**Seventy weeks.** Barnabas 16; City of God XVIII.34, XX.23.

## Summary judgment (descriptive)
Early chiliasm is well attested and Justin notes orthodox Christians who disagreed.
None of the in-library writers teaches a pre-tribulation removal of the church or two
peoples with permanently separate destinies; those distinctives are usually traced to
J. N. Darby. Early writers broadly expect the church to pass through a final
persecution under Antichrist. No council has ruled on dispensational distinctives.
