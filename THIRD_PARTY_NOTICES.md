Optional Tyndale books, TCP works and Bible editions are downloaded only on request
with their own attribution and terms. ESV/NKJV/NASB texts and provider credentials are
not included. HCF excerpts and their two candidate-link previews are withheld from
this source export. A retained HCF rights notice explains that exclusion, not permission
to repopulate the corpus. No assertion of worldwide public-domain status is made.

The legacy micrograin image is omitted pending provenance; native surfaces support
that absence. No third-party promotional screenshots, design references or system fonts
are distributed in this repository. Preserve notices when redistributing or adapting.
